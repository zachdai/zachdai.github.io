# Zach Zhanchen Dai

I am an incoming Diplôme d'Ingénieur student at [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/home). I graduated with a B.Eng. in CSE from [CUHK-Shenzhen](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en), where I worked as a research assistant at [Freedom AI](https://github.com/FreedomIntelligence) under [Dr. Yuhao Zhang](https://xiaozhang521.github.io/) on speech language models. I am currently an intern at Xiaomi's [XiaoAI](https://xiaoai.mi.com/) department, working on spatial audio understanding.

Outside of research, I enjoy listening to classical music and playing the piano. I am currently learning French ahead of my move to France.

### Selected work:
- Ongoing: Training an array-invariant, multi-channel masked autoencoder for spatial audio understanding.

- [EchoX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09174): Designed and implemented the streaming segmentation strategy for EchoX, a speech-to-speech LLM trained via a three-stage framework that mitigates the acoustic-semantic gap. The segmentation operates directly on raw LLM output embeddings, preserving end-to-end continuity and prosodic information.

- [MTalk-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18240): Built the full technical infrastructure for MTalk-Bench, a benchmark for evaluating multi-turn speech dialogue models, including the offline evaluation interface, a crowdsourcing platform on Amazon Mechanical Turk, an LLM-as-judge scoring framework, and a statistical analysis pipeline.
