A license plate deblurring system combining deep learning and traditional computer vision to restore sharp plate images from motion-blurred surveillance footage. This is a course project of EIE 4512 Digital Image Processing at CUHK-Shenzhen.

## Motivation

Motion blur caused by vehicle movement and camera shake severely degrades license plate readability in traffic surveillance systems. This project explores two complementary approaches to blind motion deblurring: a learning-based method and a classical signal processing method.

## Methods

### GAN-Based Deblurring (Pix2Pix)

We train a conditional GAN in the Pix2Pix framework:

- **Generator**: A U-Net with 5 encoder and 4 decoder blocks with skip connections, mapping blurred grayscale plates to sharp outputs at 256 $\times$ 256 resolution.
- **Discriminator**: A PatchGAN that classifies overlapping 16 $\times$ 16 patches as real or fake, encouraging local sharpness.
- **Loss**: Adversarial BCE loss + 100 $\times$ L1 pixel loss, trained with Adam (lr = $2 \times 10^{-4}$, $\beta = (0.5, 0.999)$) for 100 epochs.

Training data is generated by applying synthetic motion blur (random degree and angle) to sharp license plate crops extracted from streetscape images via perspective transforms.

### Traditional CV Deblurring

For the non-learning baseline, we perform blind estimation of motion blur parameters $(x, y)$ via basin-hopping (simulated annealing), then apply a Wiener filter for deconvolution. The optimisation uses a composite objective combining:

- Canny edge density
- Laplacian magnitude and variance
- Frequency-domain stripe penalty (to suppress ringing artefacts)

### License Plate Detection

A ResNet-18 with a modified FC head regresses 4 corner coordinates (8 values) from full streetscape images, enabling automatic plate localisation and perspective correction.

## Results

The GAN approach produces visually sharper outputs and generalises well to unseen blur patterns. The traditional method requires no training data but is slower and more sensitive to the choice of objective weights. See the [project report](https://github.com/zachdai/license-plate-deblurrer/blob/main/doc/report.pdf) for detailed quantitative comparisons.
